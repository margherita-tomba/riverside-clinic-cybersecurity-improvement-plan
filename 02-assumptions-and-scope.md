# Assumptions and scope

## Assumptions and constraints

- No internal cybersecurity staff
- Limited time for staff training
- Limited tolerance for EHR downtime during business hours
- Reliance on third-party vendors
- Staff use clinic-owned laptops/desktops
- The EHR and billing systems are cloud-hosted
- The clinic handles sensitive patient and billing information
- The clinic has limited ability to tolerate downtime during business hours.
- Improvements should be achievable within 6–12 months

## Scope and limitations

This project is based on a fictional healthcare clinic and does not include:
- medical device security assessment,
- regulatory compliance validation,
- penetration testing,
- cloud configuration review,
- or technical vulnerability scanning.

The project focuses primarily on governance, operational resilience, and practical cybersecurity improvements suitable for a small healthcare provider.
